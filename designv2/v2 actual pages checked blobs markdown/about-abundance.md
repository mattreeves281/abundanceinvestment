# About Abundance

Our business set out in 2012 with one intention: to show that the real power of money is in the
hands of individuals. Today we help thousands of people invest their money to deliver real positive
change.

## Our story

Back in 2012, we launched the first investment platform that would allow anyone to directly invest in
green projects from £5 (something commonplace today, but revolutionary at the time).

We helped pioneer the idea that ethical investment is as important for ordinary investors as wealthy
philanthropists. We spent the next decade raising over £150m for pioneering green businesses across
the country, including becoming a major funder for the UK tidal industry. And now we are excited to be
embarking on the next stage of our journey, helping to transform communities through our municipal
investments.

## Making a real impact on our places

Since 2020 we have been working with councils to launch investments that let everyone have a stake in
creating positive change in the places we live and work. The idea is a simple one. Citizens can lend
money to councils, and help finance real projects. That means every pound invested builds something
real and long-lasting, and helps people become more connected to the places they care about.

In this era of global financial markets and opaque cryptocurrencies, we believe in connecting people
with what their money is really doing is more important than ever. Thousands of people are already
investing in councils with us, and we are only just getting started.
