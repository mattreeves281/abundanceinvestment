## Investment connected to place

One of the strengths of this model is that it makes local authority borrowing more visible.
Investors are not only receiving a fixed return — they are backing projects rooted in a real
borough, with outcomes people can recognise.

That gives the page an important second role: helping explain what is being financed and why
that matters locally.
