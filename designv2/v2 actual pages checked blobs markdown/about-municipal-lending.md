## Creating a new generation of citizen investors

For generations, the idea of being an investor in a town or city was a commonplace idea. Thousands of ordinary people - many of whom were unable to open bank accounts, were able to save for their future with their local authority. The benefits were obvious; making a return to help fund their families' futures while at the same time helping create the local civic infrastructure that made the places they lived function properly.

These citizen investors long predated the outdated stereotypes that we associate with investors today. These were ordinary people who wanted to take control of their financial futures and make the places they live better. And now our municipal investments are making it possible for citizens to invest in their places once again.

### What citizen investors are saying

When we talk to people who have invested with us, it is clear the value they place on being able to connect their money with real impact in their communities.

“I’ve actually stood in places where the work has been done and thought: my money helped make this happen. That’s a really good feeling.”

It also offers a way for people to connect more deeply with their place and give something back.

“I’ve received a lot of help myself. Being able to put something back — whether that’s time or money — really matters to me.”

We also see people starting to change how they think about their council, and the role local government plays in transforming places for the better.

“It’s changed how I see the council. It’s not just something in the background — it feels like a partnership.”

### Creating a local NS&I

Over the last 160 years, the national Government has steadily built a way for people to invest in
government activity. National Savings & Investments (NS&I) underpins the nation’s finances, providing 8%
of Government borrowing. Millions of citizens save and invest through premium bonds and its other
financial products.

We want to create an NS

&I;

for local government, to let citizens take the central role in place-based
finance that they once did - and share the benefits.

## How municipal lending works

Councils have two primary types of outgoings: day to day spending - like social care, education, waste
management - and long term capital investment - like green and social infrastructure, housing and
transport. Up to 20% of council budgets are typically capital investment.

There is a large amount of capital investment each year: on average, councils have borrowed around £9
billion a year from the Public Works Loan Board, which is the Government backed institution that is
usually used for this type of borrowing.

Our municipal loans are another way for councils to borrow money for these project investments. Our
vision is to create a future where more and more of these funds come from engaged citizens with a real
stake in the future of their communities.

### Municipal lending FAQs

Can councils use the money raised to fund day to day budget holes?

No. Any municipal loan from Abundance must be used for green and social projects, in line
with the Green & Social Loan Principles.

---

Why would a council use this way of borrowing money?

We ensure that our municipal investments are good value for councils while also providing a
competitive return for investors. Councils also appreciate the opportunity to create
engagement with residents and help everyone better understand the role of local government,
which issuing an investment like this can really support.

---

Is lending money to a council risky?

No, in fact it is one of the lowest risk investments you can make. There is no record of a
council ever defaulting on debt repayment. Learn more

## Effective action begins locally

It may surprise you that locally led climate initiatives are often far more effective than national
strategies. Research shows that local approaches can have three times more impact per pound spent, and
local councils influence anywhere between 30-82% of UK carbon emissions.

Its exactly this type of action you can support when you invest in our municipal investments. You will
be helping UK councils deliver their local plans to create more resilient, sustainable and attractive
communities.
