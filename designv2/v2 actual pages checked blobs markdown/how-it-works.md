## Benefits at a glance

Our municipal investments are long term investments that you can use like other low-risk investments -
such as NS&I - and fixed rate savings products (learn more about how they compare). And they are ISA
eligible, meaning you can invest through an Innovative Finance ISA for tax-free returns.

They offer competitive rates of return - letting your money keep pace with the rising cost of living -
and they pay fixed cash interest payments every six months across the investment term, which is
usually 5 years. See how the returns work.

Our flexible approach lets you choose how you want to invest. You can pick your own
investments, and decide how you use those regular interest payments: either reinvest in new municipal
investments to keep growing your money, or withdraw for free as income.

Or you can let our automatic investment option keep your money invested and working to creating an
income for you while improving the places we live and work.

### Investing FAQs

What are the risks of municipal investments?

As with all investments, there are risks. The principal risks relate to the fact that the
investments are long term, and so interest rates may change during the investment term. See
more detail here.

---

Do your investments have FSCS cover?

No. In relation to claims against failed regulated firms, the Financial Services
Compensation Scheme (FSCS) does not cover investments in P2P loans like our council
investments. Learn more about FSCS.

---

Can I invest through an ISA?

Yes, our investments are Innovative Finance ISA eligible. Learn more about ISA investing.

---

What is the minimum investment?

£5 is the minimum investment for any of our municipal investments.

---

Are there any fees?

No, we don’t charge any fees to investors. That means free investments, deposits and
withdrawals - and the rates we quote are the rates you will receive. It is also free to use
our marketplace if you need to. We instead charge a fee to the councils who arrange
investments through us.

---

Do bigger investors get a better deal?

No, every investment is made on the same terms. There are no special deals or better rates
if you invest a larger amount. Creating inclusive investment options that are fair to
everyone is a core part of what we do.

---

Can I sell my investment if I need to exit early?

Yes, we operate a marketplace where you can list your investment for sale if you want to exist
before the term ends. But be aware that selling is not guaranteed, and you might not be able
to sell for the price you want. Find out more here.

### Interest rate FAQs

How do you decide the interest rate?

The rate is closely linked to the general cost of council borrowing on the day the loan
launches. We look at the rate of interest charged by the Public Works Loan Board, which is
the usual source of council borrowing, and ensure the interest rate on the investment is
value for money for the council on that day.

---

Are these investments an expensive way for councils to borrow money?

No. We set our interest rates for our loans to ensure that councils are not paying more to
borrow from us than the Public Work Loan Board, which is the usual source of council
lending. This ensures our investments are value for money for councils and council tax
payers.

---

Will interest rates always be competitive?

We set our interest rates to offer both good value for money for councils, and a fair rate
to investors. Council borrowing costs are related to general market interest rates, which
are the basis of the interest rates of other similar saving and investment products in the
market. This means that, to date, our municipal investments have usually been among the best
interest rates available (for similar investment terms) on the day they launch, and we would
expect that to continue.

---

Is the rate I see the rate I will be paid?

Yes. We don’t charge any fees to investors, so the interest rate quoted is what can expect
to receive.

## How our municipal investments compare

Our municipal investments are a good option for savers and investors looking for a way to create a
long term low-risk income who also want to see their money making a positive difference. If you are looking to compare the features of our investments to other low-risk options, here are some things you should think about.

### Municipal investments vs fixed rate savings

Investments are different from savings

Savings accounts are cash deposit products, with FSCS protection. Any investment involves some risks, so make sure you understand the risks of investing with us before you proceed.

Similar to fixed rate savings

Our municipal investments offer similar features to a fixed term savings account. Both pay fixed returns over the long term, and have tax free ISA options available. Our investments do have some benefits, such as the option to sell your investment early, which is not typically available with fixed rate savings products.

See what your money is doing

Because they are investments, you can see directly what you money is funding when you invest with us. This is not usually possible on cash saving products.

## Automatic investment

Automatic investment is a convenient option for investors who want to make sure their money is always
working to earn returns and help deliver positive change.

When you enable automatic investment for your account, it means that any money you deposit will be
automatically split equally across open municipal investments. It is up to you whether you deposit
using a regular standing order, or make one-off deposits when it suits you. When you receive
investment returns, these will also be reinvested in the same way.

And it is simple to turn off automatic investment in your account. So if you want to start withdrawing
your returns rather than reinvesting them, that is easy to do.

### Benefits of automatic investment

#### Automatically invest deposits

When you add money to your Abundance account it is spread evenly across open municipal investments.

#### Interest payments invested

Your regular interest payments will also be swept into open investments in the same way.

#### Keep your money working

Your money will always be earning a return for you and helping fund green and social projects.

### Automatic investment FAQs

How do you decide which investments my money will go into?

We automatically split your money evenly across all municipal investments that are open. So if there are three investments open, they will each receive a third of your money.

---

Is there a minimum investment for automatic investment?

The minimum investment is £5. That means if your balance is below £5 we will not automatically invest it.

---

What happens if there are no open investments available?

We will automatically invest your money as soon as new investments launch. It will remain in your account as cash until then.

---

I’m interested in investing more in a specific council, can I do that?

Yes, you can decide to make additional investments into any single municipal investment whenever you want. Simply follow the ‘Pick your own’ option at checkout, and deposit your payment using the specific reference number provided. Your automatic investment preferences will not be affected by this.

---

When will my money be automatically invested?

We check your account balance overnight every day. Providing the balance is greater than £5, your money will be automatically invested. If your balance is less than £5, we will try again the following day.

---

How do I turn off automatic investment?

You can change your automatic investment settings by following the link on your dashboard after you log in to your Abundance account. Any changes you make will be reflected immediately.

---

Can I customise my automatic investments?

No, at the moment we only offer the option to spread your money equally across all open municipal investments.
