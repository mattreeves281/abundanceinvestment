## How municipal investments work

Municipal investments are a simple, direct investment. You lend money to UK councils. In return
you are paid regular fixed interest payments, and you help to finance essential green and social
projects to improve our communities.

### Invest from £5

A low minimum investment, so it’s open to everyone

### Finance real projects

Your money funds real green and social projects

### Regular interest payments

Withdraw as income or reinvest to grow your pot
