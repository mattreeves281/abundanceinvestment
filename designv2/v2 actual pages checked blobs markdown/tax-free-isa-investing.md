## An innovative way to use your ISA

There are a number of different types of ISA available, the most popular ones being Cash, Stocks & Shares, and Innovative Finance ISAs. Our municipal investments are Innovative Finance ISA eligible.

You can currently deposit up to £20,000 each tax year into any mix of these ISAs with any providers, although those rules are changing from April 2027. All the investment returns you receive will be tax free, and your original invested capital also remains tax free while it stays inside the ISA system.

### Benefits of ISA investing

Investing through an ISA offers a range of benefits for savers and investors. You can get more details on the ISA rules in our ISA help centre.

#### Invest up to £20,000 tax free

You get a new ISA allowance each tax year, which lets you invest tax free for as long as your money remains in the ISA system.

#### Open as many ISAs as you like

You can now open ISA accounts of any type with any number of providers each tax year, as long as you don't exceed your total allowance.

#### Transfer existing ISAs

If you have ISA savings with other providers from previous tax years, you can transfer them to Abundance to invest in municipal investments.

### Flexible ISA benefits make it easier to manage your pot

Our Innovative Finance ISA is a flexible ISA. This means you can withdraw any uninvested money from your ISA and put it back into the same ISA later in the year without losing any of your allowance.
Learn more about flexible ISAs.

### ISA investing FAQs

How do I set up an ISA account with Abundance?

When you invest, you are given the option of investing through an Innovative Finance ISA.
If you haven’t set up an ISA with us before, you will be able to do so as part as the
investment process. We’ll just need your National Insurance number.

---

Is this a cash ISA?

No. Our ISA is an Innovative Finance ISA. It is a way of investing in our municipal
investments tax free. Cash ISAs are a deposit product, and work very differently.

---

Do the planned ISA rule changes affect the Abundance IF ISA?

No. From 2027/28, the under 65s will now only be able to deposit up to £12,000 per tax
year into Cash ISAs. But our ISA is an Innovative Finance ISA, so it isn’t affected by
this change.

---

Is investing through an ISA a lower risk option?

No. Investing through an IF ISA is just a way of getting tax-free returns. It does not
affect the risk of the underlying investments you are making.

---

Can I withdraw cash from my ISA portfolio?

Yes, you can withdraw money from your ISA free of charge. But be aware that if you do not
return the money you withdraw to your ISA before the end of the year (using the flexible
ISA rules) you will lose that ISA allowance.

---

How much can I invest in an ISA?

You can deposit £20,000 across all the ISAs you hold in each tax year.

---

How many different ISAs can I have?

You can have as many ISAs as you like with different providers each tax year. But you need
to make sure you do not deposit more than the total ISA allowance each year.

---

Can I transfer cash from another ISA to Abundance?

Yes, you can transfer cash from other ISAs to your IF ISA with Abundance to invest in
councils. You can also transfer any uninvested cash in your Abundance IF ISA to another
ISA provider if you wish. Learn more

---

Do you have FSCS cover when you invest in your ISA?

No. The ISA does not change the fact that our investments do not have FSCS cover. Learn
more.
