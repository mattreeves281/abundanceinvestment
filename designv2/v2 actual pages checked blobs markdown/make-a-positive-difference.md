## Clear and transparent lending principles

Our municipal investments make a real world impact. But you don’t have to take our word for it: we
have years of examples from projects to back that up.

Our investments are also aligned to the Green & Social Loan Principles, a set of internationally
recognised standards used by the UK Government, which ensure that any claim about green or social
impact can be backed up. These guidelines also dictate the projects that funds can be spent on, and
how we monitor councils’ spending.

We send detailed reports throughout the investment term, so you can always see exactly how your money
is helping to improve communities. The rules that govern this process are set out in our Green and
Social Finance Framework.

### Green & Social Finance Framework

The Green & Social Finance Framework framework describes the rules and processes for councils to issue a municipal investment with us, as well as Abundance’s monitoring and assurance process.

## Project criteria

In order for a council to use money raised from a municipal investment for a project, it must fall into one of the defined green & social project categories below. These categories cover a variety of project investment that makes places
more resilient, attractive and cleaner to live in, and improve the lives of residents. You can read full details in the Green & Social Finance Framework.

### Eligible green projects

Renewable Energy

This category covers new green energy and renewable heating initiatives, from solar panels
on schools to heat pumps and district heating networks.

---

Energy Efficiency

From improving the efficiency of lighting systems, or insulating leaky buildings, this
category covers schemes that reduce energy use.

---

Clean transportation

Includes schemes that help residents get around in greener ways. That might include
providing public EV charging infrastructure, or upgrading facilities to encourage cycling
and active transport.

---

Pollution Prevention and Control

Projects that promote waste reduction, encourage recycling, or reduce air or water pollution
fall within this category.

---

Climate Change Adaptation

Schemes that help residents adapt to climate change, including community infrastructure to
deal with more extreme weather events such as floods and heat.

---

Living and Natural Resources

This category covers living resources. It includes greening projects, such as development of
parks and schemes that promote biodiversity.

---

### Eligible social projects

Schools and Children’s Services

Funds can be used for capital investment, such as building renovation, equipment and
facilities, but not for day-to-day expenses.

---

Adult social care

Funds can be used for capital investment - such as building renovation, equipment and
facilities but not for day-to-day expenses.

---

Public Realm

This includes development of public spaces including parks as well as enhancing footpaths
and roads.

---

Regeneration

Schemes to regenerate areas or communities, designed to boost economic activity, provide
jobs and enhance community wellbeing.

---

Housing

Development of social housing, where works cannot be classified as one of our Eligible Green
Projects.

---

Corporate Services

Initiatives to increase efficiency, like improving internal systems, and therefore freeing
up money for front-line services.
