How it works

A simple way to invest in real places

Whether you choose investments yourself or invest regularly, the model is built to connect your money to visible projects and long-term local outcomes.

1

Choose how you want to invest

Pick individual investments yourself, or set up a regular plan to invest automatically.

2

Your money goes into real local projects

Investments help finance councils and the projects they deliver, from clean energy to greener transport and stronger communities.

3

Receive fixed returns over the term

Many investments pay income every six months, with returns that can be taken as cash or reinvested.
