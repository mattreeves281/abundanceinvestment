Important information

It is important to understand that offers to buy and sell made on the Abundance marketplace do not constitute an agreement; they are simply expressions of interest. The seller and buyer will need to discuss the offer via email or any other means in order to come to a binding agreement. The binding agreement can take many forms, from an email to a full-blown written document, but it must be in writing.

To update the investment register we need confirmation from both the seller and buyer that an agreement has been made. It is important that you record in writing the evidence of the agreement so that should a dispute arise regarding the transaction there is evidence of what was agreed.

Where a trade is agreed in writing in relation to a P2P loan, you will be electronically agreeing to a transfer of the loan as per the Transfer Certificate available here.

Investments are long term and may be hard to sell. Municipal investments are not a savings account and you are lending money to a council. Changes in market interest rates may affect the value of your investment if you sell before maturity. Abundance’s service in relation to council investments (P2P loans) is not covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may be subject to change in the future.
