An innovative way to use your ISA

There are a number of different types of ISA available, the most popular ones being Cash, Stocks & Shares, and Innovative Finance ISAs. Our municipal investments are Innovative Finance ISA eligible.

You can currently deposit up to £20,000 each tax year into any mix of these ISAs with any providers, although those rules are changing from April 2027. All the investment returns you receive will be tax free, and your original invested capital also remains tax free while it stays inside the ISA system.

Benefits of ISA investing

Investing through an ISA offers a range of benefits for savers and investors. You can get more details on the ISA rules in our ISA help centre.

1

Invest up to £20,000 tax free

You get a new ISA allowance each tax year, which lets you invest tax free for as long as your money remains in the ISA system.

2

Open as many ISAs as you like

You can now open ISA accounts of any type with any number of providers each tax year, as long as you don't exceed your total allowance.

3

Transfer existing ISAs

If you have ISA savings with other providers from previous tax years, you can transfer them to Abundance to invest in municipal investments.

Flexible ISA benefits make it easier to manage your pot

Our Innovative Finance ISA is a flexible ISA. This means you can withdraw any uninvested money from your ISA and put it back into the same ISA later in the year without losing any of your allowance. Learn more about flexible ISAs.
