Municipal investment news

University of Leeds report

The University of Leeds report explores the social case for CMIs, considering, amongst other things, how resident investors’ views of their council change post investment.

Headline finding: 55% of local investors say they trust councils that issue CMIs more than those who do not.

Read the report

Green Finance Institute report

The Green Finance Institute report explores the financial case for CMIs. Using historic PWLB borrowing and historic saving rates, as well as interviews with council treasury managers, it identifies credible use cases for CMIs today and a pathway for growing the model to scale.

Headline finding: the report suggests that, at scale, total cost of capital for councils could get consistently close to the Gilt Rate.

Read the report

CMIs deliver local government funding below Gilt Rate

During the March to June window, £1.70m was raised across the period with an average weighted saving of 81 basis points on the PWLB Certainty Rate, effectively below the Gilt Rate.

The window was priced 24 hours before the start of the Iran war, which then drove a steep increase in the cost of borrowing for councils. This demonstrates the diversification and resilience benefits of growing a citizen investor base for local government, generating the same benefits NS&I provides central government.
