What do you need to do?

When do you need to do this by?

You have 5 days to agree a sale and inform Abundance. If you don’t, the offer will expire and we will return the reserved payment to the buyer and the seller will be able to re-list the investment again on the marketplace.

If a trade is not concluded within 5 days of the buyer contacting the seller to express their interest, the offer is automatically expired. This is to ensure the buyer’s money is not indefinitely reserved and allow the seller to re-list the investment for sale in the situation where the buyer and seller have not come to an agreement.

For more information, please visit our marketplace FAQs.

Important information

It is important to understand that offers to buy and sell made on the Abundance marketplace do not constitute an agreement; they are simply expressions of interest. The seller and buyer will need to discuss the offer via email or any other means in order to come to a binding agreement. The binding agreement can take many forms, from an email to a full-blown written document, but it must be in writing.

To update the investment register we need confirmation from both the seller and buyer that an agreement has been made. It is important that you record in writing the evidence of the agreement (for example, saving the email when an agreement is reached) so that should a dispute arise regarding the transaction there is evidence of what was agreed.

Where a trade is agreed in writing in relation to a P2P loan (any council investment on Abundance from 2021), you will be electronically agreeing to a transfer of the loan as per the Transfer Certificate available here.
