Submit your annual borrower declaration

Every year, we ask all councils that have outstanding loans on Abundance to submit a declaration of compliance with the terms of the loans. This is an important part of Abundance's borrower monitoring. Please read the declarations below and confirm that they are true and correct in relation to all of the council's outstanding loans with Abundance. The declaration should be completed by the Section 151 officer or another authorised person at the council.

Declarations

The Section 151 officer, or other authorised person, confirms the following:

I confirm that:

  1. The Council is in compliance with the Representations and Warranties set out in Schedule 1 of the Loan Conditions and the Positive Undertakings set out in Schedule 2 of the Loan Conditions.
  2. The Council maintains an adequate level of reserves and provisions and has an appropriate strategy for managing its reserves over the medium term.
  3. The Council has not issued a Section 114 notice and does not expect to in the next year.
  4. The Council has not had a liquidity stress event in the past 12 months and sees no reason for one in the coming 12 months.
  5. The Council maintains an up-to-date register of Eligible Green Projects and the council funding sources that have been applied to those Projects.
  6. The Council has not disposed of any of the assets linked to the Eligible Green Projects funded by this loan.
  7. Any loan money that has been drawn down and not yet allocated is either:
    • held in temporary investment instruments that are cash, or cash equivalent instruments, within a treasury function; or
    • held in temporary investment instruments that do not include greenhouse gas intensive projects which are inconsistent with the delivery of a low carbon and climate resilient economy; or
    • applied to temporarily reduce indebtedness of a revolving nature before being redrawn for investments or disbursements to eligible assets.

Declaration 1 refers to the Representations and Warranties and Positive Undertakings in the Loan Conditions. You can read these in full below.

The Borrower represents and warrants to each of the Lenders on the Offer Open Date and annually on 30 June thereafter that:

  • Status: it is a local authority, duly established and validly existing under the laws of either England and Wales or Scotland and it has full power to own its assets and discharge its functions;
  • Binding Obligations: the obligations expressed to be assumed by it in relation to any Loan Contract are legal, valid, binding and enforceable obligations;
  • Non-conflict with other obligations: the entry into and performance by it of any Loan Contract does not and will not conflict with any law or regulation applicable to it (including, without limitation, in the case of a Borrower that is a local authority established under the laws of England and Wales, the Local Government Act 1972, Local Government Act 2003 and Localism Act 2011 and, in the case of a Borrower that is a local authority established under the laws of Scotland, the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, the Local Government in Scotland Act 2003 and the Local Authority (Capital Finance and Accounting) (Scotland) Regulations 2016), its constitutional documents or any agreement or instrument binding upon it or any of its assets;
  • Power and Authority: it has the power to enter into, perform and deliver, and has taken any and all necessary action to authorise its entry into, performance and delivery of, any documentation necessary to enter into any Loan Contracts;
  • Validity and admissibility in evidence: it has obtained all required Authorisations to enable it to enter into, exercise its rights and comply with its obligations under any Loan Contracts. Any such Authorisations are in full force and effect;
  • Governing law and enforcement: the choice of governing law in these Loan Conditions will be recognised and enforced in its jurisdiction of establishment and residence and any judgment obtained in England or Wales in relation to these Loan Conditions and/or any Loan will be recognised and enforced in that jurisdiction;
  • Insolvency: no civil action, legal proceeding or other procedure or step described in the insolvency provisions of the Loan Conditions has been taken or, to its knowledge, threatened in relation to it which would or might reasonably be expected to have a Material Adverse Effect;
  • No default: no Event of Default is continuing or is reasonably likely to result from the entry into or the performance of its obligations under any Loan Contract by it;
  • Information: to the best of its knowledge and belief (having taken all reasonable care to ensure it is so) all information that it has given in connection with the Loan Opportunity was true and accurate in all material respects as at the date it was provided, and no information has been omitted or withheld that results in that information being untrue or misleading in any material respect;
  • No breach of laws: it has not breached any law or regulation where breach would have a Material Adverse Effect.

Subject to the terms of these Loan Conditions, the Borrower undertakes to the Lenders as follows:

  • the Borrower must promptly obtain, comply with and do all that is necessary to maintain in full force and effect any Authorisation required under any law or regulation of England and Wales or Scotland (as appropriate) that enables it to perform its obligations under these Loan Conditions and/or any Loan and to ensure the legality, validity, enforceability or admissibility in evidence of these Loan Conditions and/or any Loan or that enable it to own its assets and carry on its business and carry out its functions as they are being conducted, except where failure to obtain or effect such Authorisations would not materially adversely impair its ability to perform its payment obligations under these Loan Conditions to which it is expressed to be a party;
  • the Borrower must comply with any law or regulation to which it is subject where such breach would materially adversely affect its ability to perform its obligations under these Loan Conditions or result in a liability against it in an amount which would or might reasonably be expected to have a Material Adverse Effect; and
  • the Borrower shall, promptly following request in writing from Abundance as agent for the Lenders, supply Abundance with such financial information concerning its business, assets or financial condition as Abundance as agent for the Lenders may reasonably request and shall, promptly following publication, provide Abundance as agent for the Lenders with a copy of each of its annual accounts.
