Creating benefits for councils from day one

Building a community of citizen investors takes time, but the benefits of launching a municipal investment programme start on day one. Our experience shows that you can expect to save money on your borrowing costs , and reach a broad demographic of residents with a product they will find relevant and engaging.

“It’s changed how I see the council. It’s not just something in the background — it feels like a partnership.”

Citizen investor, Hammersmith & Fulham Council

You will also be starting a conversation with citizens about how they engage with the places they care about, and beginning to deepen their understanding of the value of local government.

Building trust by shining a light on real delivery

Trust is integral to local government, and finding practical ways to grow that trust are essential. Our municipal investments let you showcase what you are delivering to create a thriving future for your communities. Our data show that citizens find municipal investments are a powerful way to build trust in their councils and to create a sense of partnership, along with deeper engagement in their local area.
