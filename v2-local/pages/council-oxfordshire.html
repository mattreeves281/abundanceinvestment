Leading climate action across the county

The council are committed to becoming carbon neutral in their estate and operations by 2030. In practice, this means they are working on a series of initiatives and projects that will reduce their operational emissions as much as possible by the year 2030 - seeking to become carbon neutral as a stepping stone ahead of the national 2050 net-zero target. They hope to show what can be achieved and inspire residents and local businesses to join them on this rewarding journey.
