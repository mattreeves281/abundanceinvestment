Award recognition for our municipal investment communications

19 June 2026

Published by Abundance Investment

We were delighted to pick up a Silver Award at the Bauer Outdoor Media Awards in the Effectiveness - SME Advertising category.

This award was for our campaign to support investments in Southwark, Hounslow, Greenwich and Hammersmith & Fulham in June 2025. This hard hitting campaign was designed to start a conversation with residents about investing in their place, build awareness of the citizen investment programmes, and build the investor community.

These prestigious awards recognise the best of out of home advertising , judged by a panel of industry experts.

It is fantastic to see our communications campaign being recognised for its effectiveness. We are looking forward to doing more of this in the future: taking the message out to communities that they can invest in their council to help make their place better.

Karl Harder, Co-founder and Joint Managing Director, Abundance
