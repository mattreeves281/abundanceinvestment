Terminology defitions

Local investors

These are people linked to an issuing council, either because they were council-recruited or because they live in an issuing council area.

Category investors

These people have no apparent connection to an issuing council. They do not live in a place that has issued a CMI, and did not mention a council when they signed up.

New vs legacy

This distinction is important for local investors. A legacy investor is a person who had already signed up for Abundance prior to their council issuing a CMI, so they have an existing brand relationship with us. New investors signed up after their council issued a CMI.

Council only / company + council

This disinction is important for category investors. People who hold company investments as well as council investments, have brand relationship pre CMI and have some particular behaviours to consider (e.g. reinvestment of company investment returns). People with only council investments have a clean brand relationship around councils.

IMD buckets

For local investors we see distinct behavioural differences between people that live in areas not traditionally associated with investing (deciles 1-5) vs those in higher deciles (6-10). No such distinction exists in category investors, so this data is only presented for local cohorts.

£250+/<£250

This is the average investment per council for a user. Our analysis has shown that <£250 is associated with more of a supporter mentality towards the CMI model, rather than an overt financial needs based decision. The motivations behind this need to be understood, but the data does clearly split along these lines.
