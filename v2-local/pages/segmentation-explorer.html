Investor segmentation explorer

Aggregated view of local and category investor segments, with lifetime sales, council depth and recent engagement signals.

Use this page to explore the main council investor segments, compare their value and engagement, and test how the proposed action segments behave against the wider investor base.

How to read this

Local investors

People linked to an issuing council, either because they were council-recruited or because they live in an issuing council area.

Category investors

Non-local council investors. Their behaviour is read as engagement with the council investment category rather than one place.

New vs legacy

New local investors signed up after their council's first investment opened. Legacy local investors already had an Abundance relationship.

Council only

Category investors whose council investments are not paired with company investments in the current segmentation view.

Company + council

Category investors who also hold company investments, often pointing to a broader existing Abundance portfolio.

£250 split

Average lifetime council investment per council. The lower band is used as a proxy for lower-ticket participation behaviour.
