How it works

Municipal investments are low-risk loans to UK councils. Councils use these funds to improve the local area, while offering you a competitive and stable long-term income.

Benefits at a glance

Our municipal investments are long-term and low-risk. They’re similar to other low-risk investments, such as NS&I and fixed rate savings products (learn more about how they compare). And they are ISA eligible, meaning you can invest through an Innovative Finance ISA for tax-free returns.

They offer competitive rates of return, letting your money keep pace with the rising cost of living, and they pay fixed cash interest payments every six months across the investment term, which is usually 5 years. See how the returns work.

Our flexible approach lets you choose how you want to invest. You can pick your own investments, and decide how you use those regular interest payments: either reinvest in new municipal investments to keep growing your money, or withdraw for free as income.

Or you can let our automatic investment option keep your money invested and working to creating an income for you while improving the places we live and work.

How our municipal investments compare

Our municipal investments are a good option for savers and investors looking for a way to create a long term low-risk income who also want to see their money making a positive difference. If you are looking to compare the features of our investments to other low-risk options, here are some things you should think about.

Municipal investments vs fixed rate savings

1

Investments are different from savings

Savings accounts are cash deposit products, with FSCS protection. Any investment involves some risks, so make sure you understand the risks of investing with us before you proceed.

2

Similar to fixed rate savings

Our municipal investments offer similar features to a fixed term savings account. Both pay fixed returns over the long term, and have tax free ISA options available. Our investments do have some benefits, such as the option to sell your investment early, which is not typically available with fixed rate savings products.

3

See what your money is doing

Because they are investments, you can see directly what you money is funding when you invest with us. This is not usually possible on cash saving products.

Municipal investments vs National Savings & Investments (NS&I)

1

Lending to government

NS&I offer a range of saving and investment products, backed by the Treasury. When you use NS&I you are lending money to the national government. With municipal investments, you are also lending to the public sector - but in this case it is local government.

2

Both are low risk options

For both options the risk of losing your money is very low. NS&I is backed by the Treasury, and municipal investments are back by councils. We discuss this topic in more detail here.

3

Municipal investments have some unique benefits

Our municipal investments offer some distinct financial benefits over NS&I. The investments are ISA eligible, pay regular cash interest payments, and have the ability to sell early if you need to.

Automatic investment

Automatic investment is a convenient option for investors who want to make sure their money is always working to earn returns and help deliver positive change.

When you enable automatic investment for your account, it means that any money you deposit will be automatically split equally across open municipal investments. It is up to you whether you deposit using a regular standing order, or make one-off deposits when it suits you. When you receive investment returns, these will also be reinvested in the same way.

And it is simple to turn off automatic investment in your account. So if you want to start withdrawing your returns rather than reinvesting them, that is easy to do.
