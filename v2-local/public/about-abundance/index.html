About Abundance

Our business set out in 2012 with one intention: to show that the real power of money is in the hands of individuals. Today we help thousands of people invest their money to deliver real positive change.

Our story

Back in 2012, we launched the first investment platform that would allow anyone to directly invest in green projects from £5. A low minimum investment is more common today, but it was revolutionary at the time. We spent the next decade raising over £135m for pioneering green businesses across the country - including wind, solar and energy efficiency - and becoming a major funder for the UK tidal industry.

We are proud to have helped pioneer the idea that that we should all take an interest in how our money is used, not just the returns we make.

Making a real impact on our places

Since 2020, we have been working with councils to help improve places across the UK. The idea is a simple one. Citizens can lend money to councils and help finance real projects. That means every pound invested builds something real and long-lasting, and helps people become more connected to the places they care about.

In this era of financial markets and opaque cryptocurrencies, we believe it’s more important than ever to connect people with what their money is really doing. Thousands of people are already investing in councils with us, and we are only just getting started.
